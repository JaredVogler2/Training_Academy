# PHASE 11 — Music Lab plus opt-in enrichment rooms

Prioritize 1 high-quality music room over numerous half-built activities. Later extras should be **clearly marked experimental** until they have real authored lessons, interactions and QA.

## Music Lab (required)
Build a responsive, delightful musical workshop with a one-octave virtual keyboard, drums and rhythm strips. Progression: distinguish high/low, same/different patterns, clap/tap on a slow beat, echo 2–4 notes, recognize repeated phrase, build a simple melody. Teach pitch and timing with repeatable examples, never assume reading sheet music or having perfect pitch.

Use WebAudio or licensed instrument samples bundled with the app; satisfy mobile first-interaction audio unlock. Use audible + visual pulse feedback and a reduced-motion equivalent. Grade rhythm with generous latency windows and device calibration, not millisecond precision. Do not generate or ship copyrighted melodies; write original short phrases. Record what is actually assessed (pitch selection, sequence order, rough tempo) rather than 'musical talent'.

## Optional expansion rooms (implement only if premium)
- **Spatial Workshop:** tangram rotations, symmetry and fit without requiring shape names; mathematical transform validation.
- **Chess Nursery:** learn squares, simple piece movement, attacks and 1-step plans before full chess; no aggressive clock.
- **Sky Observatory:** day/night, moon phases and basic planet order; distinguish model scale from physical reality.
- **Language Garden:** parent-selectable language, picture-based practical words and brief phrases using native-quality licensed recordings; do not claim conversational fluency from flashcards.
- **Feelings Theatre:** recognize and discuss feelings and perspective-taking through short fictional social situations; no diagnostic labeling, no shaming.

Each optional mini-world must have explicit skill prerequisites, 5+ distinct meaningful challenges, success/error scaffolds and mobile tests before being called 'playable'. Until then create truthful locked/preview cards, not dead tap targets.

## Acceptance
Music module: original working audio, 10+ authored patterns, interactive replay, error feedback, sample-timing tests and mobile touch E2E. Document source audio licensing and sound-disabled fallback. Commit.
