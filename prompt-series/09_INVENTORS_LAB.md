# PHASE 09 — Inventor's Lab: prediction, experiment and iteration

Build a highly tactile island workshop with bright materials, charming machines and robust browser interactions. The teaching objective is **scientific reasoning**, not magic 'correct answer' selection.

## Learning loop
Observe a problem -> make a prediction -> choose materials or construct a design -> run a visible simulation -> compare prediction to result -> alter one variable -> retest -> describe what changed with picture options or optional parent-supported narration.

## Activities
1. Bridge Builder: span a gap with distinct length, support and material constraints; explain load/structure with a simplified, coherent deterministic model.
2. Ramp Race: predict how slope, friction and weight affect motion; don't claim that toy physics is a rigorous real-world simulator.
3. Balance Garden: leverage and center of mass using accessible visual objects.
4. Floating Lab: choose and compare safe everyday objects, with clear prediction vs observation.
5. Wind Shelter: design a structure to withstand a gentle virtual gust; qualitative feedback linked to support placement.

## Architecture
Simulations must be authored/tested models with explicit assumptions, invariants and calibration fixtures; avoid physically impossible behaviors or 'the answer the designer wanted' hidden inside graphics. Make material choices and outcomes genuinely consequential and repeatable. Show animated cause and effect, not silent scoring.

## Off-screen transfer
Every activity ends with an optional **parent-guided real-world challenge**, for example test which block bridge supports a toy car or predict what floats in a supervised water bowl. Include materials list, likely mess, clear adult supervision, household safety warnings (small parts/choking and water), and a 2-question observation prompt. Parent records completion; never claim app verified physical success.

## Gameplay and art
High fidelity close-up workshop, manipulable blocks, shadows, satisfying assembly and failure/reset transitions. Don't overwhelm a young child with precise drag physics; snap-to-place, large handles, undo and clear camera framing. No real flames, electricity, sharp tools or dangerous physical experiment suggestions.

## Validation
Deterministic test fixtures for design outcomes, meaningful variation from changing one variable, child can identify prediction/result, trace safe exits and generate an engineering portfolio card. Capture a phone and desktop end-to-end demonstration and document model limitations. Commit.
