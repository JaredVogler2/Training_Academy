# PHASE 10 — Story Harbor and Geography Explorer

Implement two supporting worlds as polished independent experiences, both accessible to nonreaders.

## Story Harbor
- Animated three-panel story composer: choose character, setting, goal, complication and resolution by pictures and spoken cues.
- Child sequences beginning/middle/end, predicts consequences and explains why characters act. Use multi-choice pictorial prompts; optional parent-led free narration (not a mandated microphone or AI transcript).
- Encourage original stories without scoring subjective creativity as a numeric 'intelligence' rank. Assess objectively observable sequence/cause relationships and let parent optionally record narrative participation.
- A storybook page-turn animation, beautiful illustrations and coherent character expressions are required, not stock slides.
- Deliver at least 6 authored branching story seeds with valid permutations, accessible captions for adults and voice scripts for kids.

## Geography Explorer
- A friendly stylized interactive globe/map with oceans, continents, land shapes, iconic landmarks and animations of travel; avoid implying geopolitical borders are immutable.
- Begin with spatial ideas (land/water, near/far, top/bottom, oceans/continents), then named continents, major countries and flags as optional memory enrichment.
- Never introduce geography by assuming printed-name reading. Spoken place names, clear flags, physical visual landmarks and sound prompts.
- Add optional family/culture cards based on accurate, respectful facts and reviewed neutral representations. Never stereotype people/countries through costumes or one 'typical' family.
- Include at least 7 continent/region tasks, 15 grounded country/landmark identification trials with reviewable attribution and stable content data; make content expandable.

## Quality/tests
Test story path coherence and no dead-end states; verify geography hotspots and spatial correctness, no country 'knowledge' inferred from tap-spamming or permanently visible labels. Test phone portrait globe drag, pinch alternative, orientation and keyboard. Screenshots and audio review for both worlds. Commit.
